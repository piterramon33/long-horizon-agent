# Bluepeak Foods: Supplier Quality Manual, Section 7 (Cold Chain)

This document is fictional and exists only to test the agent.

All frozen products must arrive at -18 °C or below. Chilled products are accepted
between 0 °C and 4 °C. Receiving staff measure the core temperature of every
delivery and reject frozen loads warmer than -15 °C.

Monitoring records must be kept for 1 year. Corrective actions are written on
form QA-12 and signed by the shift supervisor. Suppliers must report any
temperature excursion within 24 hours.
