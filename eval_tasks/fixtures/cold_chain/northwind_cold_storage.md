# Northwind Cold Storage: Handling Rules (rev. 4)

This document is fictional and exists only to test the agent.

- Frozen goods must stay at -18 °C or colder at all times, including during loading.
- Chilled goods must be kept between 0 °C and 4 °C.
- Trucks are pre-cooled for at least 30 minutes before loading.
- A data logger records the temperature every 15 minutes.
- Temperature records are retained for 2 years.
- Any deviation above the limit lasting more than 30 minutes is reported to the client within 24 hours.
